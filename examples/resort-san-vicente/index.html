Where the Crowd Ends

This is a place you reach by leaving the road behind. The last stretch is unpaved, and then there is only the sound of water on the shore.

There are no other resorts here, no music drifting from a neighbouring bar. What you hear at night is what was always here — wind, tide, and the occasional call of a bird.

The beach in front of you is yours for the length of your stay. No loungers lined in rows, no one selling anything. Just the sand, the reef, and the quiet.
